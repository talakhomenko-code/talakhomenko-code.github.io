About

Artist first, motion designer second.

Before After Effects there were more than ten years of sculpture and painting. I hold a Master's degree in Fine Arts from Borys Grinchenko Kyiv University (2018) — that training is why my frames are built on composition, form, light and color, not just effects. From 2019 to 2024 I also taught core disciplines to art and design students, so giving clear feedback and explaining visual decisions comes naturally.

For the last 3+ years I've been turning that eye into performance video: in-house creatives for user acquisition and employer branding, and freelance work for consumer apps and fintech products.
